Case Study 07 · Inbound Foreign Investment

Facilitating Inbound Mining Due Diligence: PARET Mining LLC

Coordinating a comprehensive Ghanaian business mission for Miami executive Dr. Rudolph Moise: state protocol at Flagstaff House, Ghana Gold Board consultations, and on-site field assays.

✦ Bilateral Delegation Miami–Accra ✦ Flagstaff House & Gold Board ✦ Aboabo No. 4 Geological Assays

The Operational Context

Foreign mining investors require rigorous due diligence, sovereign protocol assurance, and trusted on-the-ground operational counterparts before considering multimillion-dollar capital commitments in West Africa.

The Strategic Challenge

Bridging technical on-site geological sampling in Ghana's interior (Dormaa Ahenkro / Aboabo No. 4) with high-level sovereign protocol at Flagstaff House and regulatory alignment with the Ghana Gold Board.

Role & Executed Actions

Role: Lead Facilitator & Managing Director, AFA-EF Mining Ltd (June 29 – July 8, 2025).

  • Co-hosted welcoming reception and working dinners in Accra for Dr. Rudolph Moise (PARET Mining LLC, Miami) and delegates alongside Atta Frimpong Addo.
  • Facilitated bilateral economic discussions at Flagstaff House with government leadership.
  • Organized consultative session with Ghana Gold Board CEO Sammy Gyamfi on gold export regulations.
  • Coordinated interior mission travel to Sunyani and Dormaa Ahenkro, facilitating courtesy call with the Aboabo No. 4 Paramount Chief.
  • Supervised on-site geological sampling and assay tests verifying ore carats.
  • Arranged high-level commercial dinner with Ghanaian industrial leaders Chris Wilmot and Sam Jonas.

Documented Outcomes & True Status

  • Status: Opportunity explored. In strict accordance with credibility rules: The joint-venture partnership remains exploratory and is not confirmed or finalized.
  • Empirical on-site sampling completed and documented.
  • Demonstrated end-to-end capacity to guide foreign multinational delegations through Ghanaian legal, cultural, and technical ecosystems.

Core Executive Lesson

Traditional community authorities (Chiefs) and local stakeholders are equal partners alongside federal regulators. On-the-ground technical verification must always precede equity structuring.
