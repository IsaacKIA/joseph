Case Study 02 · Mining & Commodities

Formalizing Gold Value Chains with Canadian Bullion Markets

Executing bilateral agreements connecting Ghanaian Au Doré production with Toronto bullion markets, backed by strict Ghana Gold Board regulatory compliance and Odoo ERP traceability.

✦ Bilateral Agreement Toronto (April 2026) ✦ Ghana Gold Board Compliance ✦ Odoo ERP Provenance

The Operational Context

Ghana is Africa's premier gold producer. However, small-to-medium scale aggregation historically suffered from informal middlemen, inconsistent assaying standards, and limited access to institutional North American buyers. The establishment of the Ghana Gold Board compliance framework created a historic opportunity to formalize aggregation corridors.

The Strategic Challenge

Establishing an airtight compliance, logistics, and assay corridor capable of satisfying both Ghana Gold Board / Bank of Ghana export mandates and rigorous Canadian institutional bullion due diligence.

Role & Executed Actions

Role: Managing Director, AFA-EF Mining Ltd (Jan 2025–Present).

  • Negotiated and executed a formal Strategic Partnership Agreement and a Sale & Purchase Agreement for Au Doré bars with The Bullion Mart Corporation (Toronto, Canada) on April 17, 2026.
  • Embedded Odoo ERP architecture across aggregation nodes to track batch weights, purity assays, and custody handoffs.
  • Aligned aggregation protocols with Ghana Gold Board assay laboratories and Bank of Ghana documentation.
  • Supported responsible small-scale mining partnerships under the Equipment Pool Model.

Documented Outcomes

  • Formal execution of two milestone cross-border agreements on 17 April 2026 connecting Ghana production to North American bullion liquidity.
  • Direct institutional bridge eliminating non-accredited intermediaries.
  • Audit-ready traceability satisfying international anti-money laundering (AML) and responsible mineral sourcing standards.

Core Executive Lesson

In international commodity trade, counterparties prioritize verifiable regulatory compliance and chain-of-custody documentation over price discounts. Traceability is the ultimate liquidity multiplier.
