Operating Philosophy

Joseph operates where international business law, technological capability, and operational logistics meet tangible African development needs.

Over two decades of commercial leadership in Ghana, Joseph has built and scaled businesses that bridge grassroots execution with global counterparties. His leadership spans artificial intelligence deployment at Cognitive AI Technologies Ltd, regulatory gold aggregation and export at AFA-EF Mining Ltd, clinical oxygen and renewable energy generation at Sunbio2 Ghana Ltd, humanitarian health system coordination at PROFOH Ghana, and organic farming at Sankofa International Organic Farms.

His background pairs an MSc in International Business with Law from the University of Salford (UK) with postgraduate artificial intelligence and machine learning training from the McCombs School of Business, UT Austin. This combination allows him to bridge boardroom governance and complex algorithmic systems with operational integrity.
