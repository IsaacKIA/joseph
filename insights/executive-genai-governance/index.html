What Executives Should Ask Before Adopting Generative & Agentic AI

A pragmatic governance checklist for board members and CEOs navigating vendor hype, regulatory exposure, and operational systems integration.

✦ Fiduciary Algorithm Risk ✦ Agentic Execution Auditability ✦ Board-Level AI Guardrails
Joseph Fabian Ayitevi Board Chairman & AI Fiduciary
· 6 min read · Draft Topic
Executive Briefing At A Glance
  • Unit Economics over Prestige: Never deploy AI without verifying whether cloud inference costs are justified by real productivity or retained margins.
  • Enterprise Data Sovereignty: Ensure internal corporate IP and customer records are never used to train third-party foundation models without indemnification.
  • Agentic Guardrails & Human-in-the-Loop: Autonomous workflows must feature deterministic boundaries, audit trails, and human sign-off on binding decisions.

Every executive team in 2026 is under intense pressure from board members and shareholders to demonstrate an "AI strategy". In the rush to look innovative, organizations frequently sign expensive enterprise contracts with AI vendors without defining what business problem is actually being solved.

Before approving an AI capital expenditure, every CEO and board member should insist on four fundamental questions:

1. What is the unit economics of this implementation? If an AI system reduces customer query times from 10 minutes to 30 seconds, does that translate into retained customers or reduced headcount, or does it merely generate cloud inferencing costs?

2. Where does our proprietary enterprise data reside? Are customer records, financial ledgers, and proprietary trade secrets being used to train third-party foundation models without compensation or indemnification?

"Never adopt artificial intelligence for marketing prestige. Insist on measurable unit-economics improvement and strict data ownership from day one."

Joseph Fabian Ayitevi · Silvercrown Commercial Board Chairman

3. What is our fallback protocol when the model hallucinates? Autonomous agentic workflows can execute erroneous database queries or issue invalid contracts if unmonitored. Human-in-the-loop oversight is a non-negotiable governance requirement.

4. Does our technical workforce understand how to maintain the pipeline? If external consultants build a black-box model and leave, your enterprise inherits technical debt rather than a strategic capability.

Responsible AI deployment is disciplined, unglamorous, and rigorously measured. That is how real competitive advantages are constructed.
