AFA-EF Mining & The Bullion Mart Corporation

June–July 2025 · Miami & Accra

PARET Mining Inbound Delegation, Ghana

Opportunity Explored

Joseph facilitated and co-hosted a high-level Ghanaian investment due diligence visit with Atta Frimpong Addo for Miami-based executive Dr. Rudolph Moise (PARET Mining LLC) and Rudolph Moise Jr. Included bilateral discussions at Flagstaff House, regulatory consultations with Ghana Gold Board CEO Sammy Gyamfi, courtesy call with the Aboabo No. 4 Paramount Chief, and geological gold assays. Potential joint-venture investment remains exploratory and unconfirmed.

April 2025 · Washington, D.C.

Washington, D.C. Bilateral Mission

Meetings Held

Mission to Washington D.C. coinciding with the World Bank and IMF Spring Meetings. Conducted bilateral congressional discussions in Room 1436 with U.S. Congressman Joe Wilson (House Armed Services / Foreign Affairs), discussing bilateral trade and receiving a commemorative goodwill gift for Ghana. Explored critical minerals, AI innovation hubs, and IMF project linkages. No commercial investment was closed during these exploratory meetings.
